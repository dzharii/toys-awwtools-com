Example 03 / Engineering policy

Make the quality bar executable.

This example creates a modest baseline for regular business applications: compiler-enforced defaults, explicit dependency direction, async and cancellation rules, risk-based tests, and release/rollback review.

Download Program.csDownload project file

Practical rather than ceremonial

The generated policy prefers a simple modular application until real ownership, deployment, or scaling needs justify more. It forbids swallowed exceptions, unbounded retries, premature microservices, and abstractions without a demonstrated boundary.
