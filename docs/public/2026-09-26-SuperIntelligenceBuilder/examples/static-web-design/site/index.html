Before you carry it over

What belongs on the bench?

Bring one clean, portable item you can carry yourself. Include its power cord, loose pieces, and anything that helps explain the problem.

⌁

Drawer A

Lamps

Table and floor lamps with loose switches, worn cords, unstable fittings, or light that flickers.

◇

Drawer B

Toys

Mechanical and simple electronic toys with stuck parts, broken joins, or battery-contact trouble.

✣

Drawer C

Clothing

Garments needing a patch, button, simple seam repair, or help deciding on a visible mend.

◉

Drawer D

Small appliances

Carryable household appliances such as toasters, fans, and kettles for diagnosis and simple repair.

A good candidate is…

Complete enough to diagnose, safe to handle, and small enough for one person to carry. A volunteer will assess it with you before any work begins.

Leave at home…

Items that are leaking, contaminated, fuel-powered, pressurized, or likely to require licensed trade work. If you are unsure, email before transporting it.

02

A shared investigation

How a repair visit works

You stay with your object and take part. The goal is not just a fix; it is leaving with a better understanding of the thing you own.

  1. 1

    Check in

    Tell us what the item does—or no longer does. We will note safety concerns and match you with a volunteer.

  2. 2

    Look closely

    You and the fixer inspect the item together, starting with the simplest likely causes.

  3. 3

    Try a repair

    If a safe repair is possible with available time, tools, and parts, you work through it side by side.

  4. 4

    Choose what is next

    Test the result. If it cannot be fixed today, leave with findings and a practical next step.

Plan a comfortable visit

Access is part of the setup.

Venue-specific routes and facilities will be posted with the event details. Here is what the team will confirm before doors open.

Step-free route
Entrance, work area, and accessible toilet details will be confirmed with the venue.
Seating + pace
Chairs will be available. You can observe, take breaks, or ask a volunteer to handle fiddly steps.
Sound + lighting
Repair spaces can be busy. A quieter time or area will be identified where the venue allows.
Communication
Written instructions and showing rather than telling are welcome. Bring a support person if useful.
