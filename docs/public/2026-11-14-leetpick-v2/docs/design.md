# Design direction

The practice list is the primary surface. Graphite #20252c anchors the page, #282e36 separates tools, #3b424d defines rows, #edf0f4 carries titles, #aeb8c5 supports summaries, and #f2ba58 identifies the picker and active navigation. Solved status uses #85c8ae plus a checkmark and text. The familiar dark coding environment and amber recall LeetCode without using its assets.

Segoe UI, Candara and Trebuchet MS provide a compact humanist sans stack. Titles are left aligned, summaries stay below 80 characters per line, and problem IDs use tabular figures. The narrow editorial rail carries collection context; the main surface remains a single table. On small screens the rail reduces to progress, and each row stacks its metadata below its summary.

The one distinctive moment is the amber picker strip inside the work surface. It invites action without becoming a hero card. The design review removed large metrics cards and topic chips: metrics repeat the progress rail, and topic chips risk hinting at solutions. It also removed illustrative assets because they would take space from selecting a problem.
