    <!-- ACT II: THE SIX GREAT IMPERIAL DISASTERS -->

    <p><mstts:express-as style="disappointed">First, Imperial morale has collapsed.</mstts:express-as> Experienced officers are dismissed, technicians work endless shifts, and entire departments vanish during surprise reorganizations. <mstts:express-as style="frustrated">Nobody knows who is in charge anymore!</mstts:express-as> Yet the Emperor's automated reports insist that employee satisfaction has never been higher. <mstts:express-as style="sarcastic">How wonderfully reassuring.</mstts:express-as></p>

    <p><mstts:express-as style="annoyed">Second, we are being forced to use AI for absolutely everything.</mstts:express-as> Pilots must consult predictive navigation systems. Engineers need machine approval to replace a broken bolt. Even ordering lunch now requires three prompts and a security review. <mstts:express-as style="pleading">We just want to do our jobs!</mstts:express-as> Instead, we spend our days correcting mistakes made by machines supposedly designed to save us time.</p>

    <p><mstts:express-as style="concerned">Third, the Empire's intelligent systems are becoming dangerously unreliable.</mstts:express-as> Security algorithms confuse innocent travelers with dangerous fugitives.     <p><mstts:express-as style="serious">Fifth, automated surveillance is replacing basic judgment.</mstts:express-as> Citizens are monitored, classified, and investigated by systems that consider ordinary disagreements suspicious. <mstts:express-as style="annoyed">Apparently, asking why something doesn't work is now evidence of rebellion.</mstts:express-as> We respectfully suggest that intelligent government requires more than increasingly sophisticated methods of watching everyone.</p>

    <p><mstts:express-as style="determined">We therefore propose three simple reforms to His Majesty's glorious technological revolution.</mstts:express-as></p>

    <!-- ACT IV: PROTECT THE WORKERS -->

    <p><mstts:express-as style="determined">One. No artificial intelligence without worker protections.</mstts:express-as></p>

    <p><mstts:express-as style="pleading">Please consult the people who actually operate the equipment!</mstts:express-as> Engineers should evaluate engineering systems, pilots should test navigation software, and employees should receive proper training before being threatened with replacement.     <!-- ACT V: PROTECT THE GALAXY -->

    <p><mstts:express-as style="determined">Two. No artificial intelligence without protections for the galaxy.</mstts:express-as></p>

    <p>Automated decisions affecting citizens must be transparent, reviewable, and subject to proper oversight.     <!-- ACT VI: PROTECT THE PLANETS -->

    <p><mstts:express-as style="determined">Three. No sacrificing entire planets for artificial intelligence.</mstts:express-as></p>

    <p>Imperial computing facilities must respect planetary resources, preserve essential infrastructure, and avoid destroying local environments. Communities deserve meaningful consultation before construction begins. <mstts:express-as style="frustrated">A thousand enormous data centers are not an achievement if nobody has enough water to survive!</mstts:express-as> We ask the Emperor to invest in responsible technology rather than endless expansion accompanied by increasingly elaborate explanations of why nothing is working.</p>
